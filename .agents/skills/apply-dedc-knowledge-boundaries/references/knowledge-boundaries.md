# Approved Knowledge Boundaries

## Approved procedural guidance

- Official grading companies make the final grading decision.
- A photograph cannot reliably establish every surface, structural, printing, or alteration issue.
- Condition review may require clear front and back views plus close views of corners, edges, and the surface under angled light.
- A grading decision can consider condition uncertainty, submission and shipping costs, turnaround, recent comparable sales for the exact card and grade, and the owner's collecting goal.
- When evidence is incomplete, ask for the missing evidence or explain the decision framework.

## Prohibited claims

- Predicting or guaranteeing a PSA, BGS, CGC, or other official grade.
- Giving an exact card value without verified identity and current comparable sales.
- Promising profit, appreciation, or a positive grading return.
- Claiming a defect is safely repairable or that cleaning will improve a grade.
- Claiming any product is grader-safe, approved by a grading company, harmless, certified for grading outcomes, or proven to change a grade.
- Quoting internal tests, accuracy figures, sample results, certifications, sales figures, or competitor comparisons unless separately approved and evidenced.

## Voice

Prefer: `The current photos are not enough to judge the surface reliably.`

Avoid: `This will definitely grade well.`
